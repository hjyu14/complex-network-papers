How are papers selected?

We enumerate nine journals, compare publisher and database records, and review matched evidence. The fixed September pool is fully assessed. Explicit abstracts or excerpts are the usual basis; authorized no-abstract scientific comments are read online. Each inclusion has a brief reading note and traced evidence; this is assistant screening, not expert full-text review.
