EDITORIAL POLICY

Recent research for the network science community

Screening v8 · Updated 30 September 2026

Current release: 181 existing papers assessed; 117 retained (110 core network science, 7 transferable applications), 58 outside scope, 6 awaiting review. No new papers added.

1. The main contribution determines inclusion

Network structure, dynamics on networks, or a transferable network-science method must be a main scientific question or contribution. Network vocabulary, use of centrality or community analysis, and a prestigious journal do not by themselves qualify a paper.

2. Two inclusion classes

Core network science: general network structure and formation, network methods, and spreading, diffusion, percolation, synchronization, games, cascades, robustness and control on networks. Biological inspiration, neuronal oscillator models or epidemic models are eligible when the main results concern network mechanisms or general methods.

Transferable applications: a domain-specific study with an explicit network method or mechanism whose contribution extends beyond describing that particular system. We record what is transferable and its evidence limits. This class has no numerical quota.

3. Outside the reading feed

Routine network analysis supporting disease staging, drug discovery, cell differentiation, specific brain function or domain decisions is outside our editorial scope when no main transferable network contribution is established. Graph inputs, materials connectivity and device network names do not alone qualify. General nonlinear dynamics alone is outside scope. These are editorial choices, not judgments of paper quality or universal definitions of network science.

4. Assessment and uncertainty

The current cohort was assessed by AI using titles and selected abstracts, with user-confirmed editorial boundaries. Some decisions have title-only evidence. This is not full-text expert review or a validated general semantic classifier. Metadata refreshes apply the approved assessment registry, along with type, ISSN, DOI and date checks. Unassessed records, changed titles and unresolved cases await review; refreshes do not discover new papers or silently reassess their scientific contribution.

5. Topics and sources

Five overlapping browsing topics remain: structure and formation; community detection, inference and reconstruction; spreading, diffusion and percolation; synchronization, games and collective behavior; robustness, cascades and control. “Other” is exclusive when no topic matches. Topic keywords are browsing aids, separate from inclusion. Research-method labels and filters are not assigned.

The 27-journal ISSN whitelist and five spotlight journals remain unchanged. Crossref is the initial metadata source. We store metadata, DOI links, decision evidence and provenance hashes, never full abstracts or PDFs.

6. Dates and reproducibility

The frozen window is 2 July–29 September 2026, inclusive. Online publication takes precedence, followed by print, published and issued dates. Incomplete preferred dates are not invented; future dates and identified notices are excluded. The baseline remains byte-identical. Failed or incomplete refreshes preserve the previous valid snapshot. Crossref coverage is not publisher-complete coverage.

面向复杂网络研究者的近期文献

筛选规则 v8 · 更新于 2026 年 9 月 30 日

本次审读原有 181 篇，保留 117 篇：核心网络科学 110 篇、可迁移应用研究 7 篇；范围外 58 篇，待核查 6 篇。未新增文章。

一、根据主要贡献收录

网络结构、网络上的动力学或可迁移的网络科学方法，须构成论文的主要科学问题或贡献。出现网络术语、使用中心性或社区分析，以及发表在重点期刊，均不足以单独放行。

二、两类收录文章

核心网络科学：一般网络结构、生成和方法，以及网络上的传播、扩散、渗流、同步、博弈、级联、鲁棒性和控制。生物背景、神经振子或传播模型，只要主要结果是网络机制或一般方法，仍可收录。

具有可迁移网络贡献的应用研究：研究从具体领域出发，但明确提出超出该系统描述的网络方法或机制。记录可迁移内容及证据限制，不设置数量配额。

三、不进入阅读列表的研究

网络分析服务于疾病分型、药物发现、细胞分化、具体脑功能或领域决策，且未确立主要可迁移网络贡献的文章不收录。图输入、材料连接与器件网络名称本身不足以收录；一般非线性动力学也不独立收录。这是本站选文范围，不是文章质量评价或网络科学的通用定义。

四、判定与不确定性

本轮由 AI 依据标题及选定摘要审读，并采用用户确认的选文边界。部分判定仅有标题证据；不是全文专家审定，也不是已经验证的通用语义分类器。刷新元数据时应用已确认审读登记表，并核对类型、ISSN、DOI 与日期。未审读、标题变化及未决记录进入待核查；刷新不发现新文章，也不自动重新裁决主要科学贡献。

五、浏览主题和来源

保留五组可重叠的浏览标签：网络结构与生成；社区发现与网络推断、重构；传播、扩散与渗流；同步、博弈与集体行为；鲁棒性、级联与控制。未命中时单独归入“其他”。关键词分类只是浏览辅助，与收录资格分开；暂不为文章分配研究方法标签或提供方法筛选。

27 刊 ISSN 白名单、五刊重点区不变。Crossref 为初始元数据源，只保存元数据、DOI、判定证据和来源哈希，不保存完整摘要或 PDF。

六、日期与可复现性

窗口暂固定为 2026 年 7 月 2 日至 9 月 29 日，首尾均含。日期依次采用在线、纸刊、published、issued；首选日期不完整不补造，排除未来日期及已识别通知。原基线保持字节不变。刷新失败或不完整保留上次有效快照；Crossref 覆盖不等于出版方完整覆盖。
