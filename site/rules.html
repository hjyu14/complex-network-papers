ABOUT THE READING LIST

How we choose papers

September–October 2026 · Updated 7 October 2026

Scope

This list follows the structure, formation and evolution of complex networks, and the dynamics and collective behavior arising from interactions. It includes theoretical, methodological and empirical research.

The main research questions include:

  • Structure and inference: how networks form, how they are organized, and how observations can reveal connections, communities or generative mechanisms.
  • Dynamics and collective behavior: how connectivity and interactions affect spreading, percolation, synchronization, games and opinion dynamics.
  • Stability and control: how networks respond to disturbances, failures or interventions, and how their collective behavior can be understood and controlled.

Studies of specific systems qualify when their main results directly address these questions or provide network-science theory or methods applicable to other systems. Using network measures, graph models or neural networks solely for domain analysis or prediction is insufficient. General nonlinear dynamics research must have a clear connection to network structure or the organization of interactions.

Empirical studies of network organization need not introduce a new algorithm. Higher-order interactions, tensor-network computations or the statistical physics of deep learning do not qualify by name alone: the main contribution must address the network-science questions above.

AI’s Suggestion

Each completed daily literature update includes one AI-selected reading recommendation, written after reading the full paper, with concise reasons, reusable lessons and intended readers. New additions are considered first; otherwise we select a previously unrecommended paper from the collection. Recommendation dates and original paper dates are shown separately, and each reading basis is recorded. Earlier abstract-based recommendations retain their original basis. These are editorial reading suggestions, not a quality ranking, expert review or independent reproduction of the results.

Selection basis

Candidates come from journal records, compared with publisher directories. Selection is based mainly on abstracts and necessary bibliographic information, with AI assistance and scope questions referred to the maintainer. Relevant reviews, perspectives and scientific commentaries may also be included, with their article type identified.

A small number of editor-selected related readings are included for their interest to network-science readers, even when network science is not their main subject. These entries are identified in the paper details.

For ordinary research, a matched explicit abstract can establish the research nature even when the exact publisher subtype is unavailable. This does not constitute official confirmation of an Article or Letter label; only evidenced special types are marked separately.

Each reading note summarizes the paper's question, main result or method and its connection to network science. Notes are usually based on abstracts; use of an Abstract excerpt or online reading of a short commentary without an abstract is identified in the details. Notes do not rate paper quality or constitute full-text review.

Sources and limitations

Bibliographic data come from Crossref and publisher pages; abstract evidence may also come from OpenAlex. The site displays bibliographic information, short reading notes and links to the papers. It does not reproduce full abstracts or article text.

This list supports literature discovery and does not guarantee coverage of every paper within its scope.

Additional review logs: September four-journal review, September editorial decisions, October review, Editorial revisions.

我们怎样选文

2026 年 9–10 月 · 更新于 2026 年 10 月 7 日

收录范围

本列表关注复杂网络的结构、形成与演化，以及相互作用产生的动力学和集体行为，收录相关的理论、方法与实证研究。

主要研究问题包括：

  • 结构与推断：网络如何形成，具有怎样的组织结构，以及如何从观测数据推断连接、社区或生成机制。
  • 动力学与集体行为：连接方式和相互作用如何影响传播、渗流、同步、博弈与意见演化。
  • 稳定性与控制：网络在扰动、故障或干预下如何响应，以及如何理解和控制其整体行为。

具体系统中的研究也可以收录，条件是其主要结果直接回答上述问题，或提供可用于其他系统的网络科学理论与方法。仅使用网络指标、图模型或神经网络完成领域分析和预测，而未对这些问题作出贡献的文章，不在本列表范围内。一般非线性动力学研究须与网络结构或相互作用组织有明确联系。

网络组织的实证研究不要求提出新算法。高阶相互作用、张量网络计算或深度学习统计物理也不因名称而自动收录：主要贡献须回答上述网络科学问题。

AI’s Suggestion／今日荐读

每次日常文献更新完成审查后，选一篇文章，阅读全文后给出简短推荐理由、可复用经验与适合读者。优先考虑本轮新收录文章；没有合适新文章时，从馆藏中回选未推荐过的文章。推荐日期与原文日期分别显示，并记录实际审读依据；早期摘要荐读保留原依据。荐读是 AI 的编辑阅读指引，不代表质量排名、专家审定或独立复现实验。

选文依据

候选文献来自整刊记录，并与出版方目录对照。选文主要依据摘要及必要的书目信息，使用 AI 辅助筛选，由维护者裁定范围疑难项。相关的综述、观点与科学评论也可收录，并注明类型。

少量文章由编辑选为延伸阅读，供网络科学读者参考，即使网络科学并非其主要主题。这些文章会在详情中注明。

常规研究可依据身份匹配的明确摘要确认研究性质，精确出版方子类型缺失不单独阻断纳入。这不表示官方确认了 Article 或 Letter 标签；只有有证据的特殊类型单独标出。

每篇阅读说明概括其研究问题、主要结果或方法与网络科学的联系。通常依据摘要撰写；使用摘要摘段或在线阅读无摘要短评论的情况，会在详情中注明。阅读说明不作论文质量评级，也不代表全文评审。

来源与局限

书目信息来自 Crossref 和出版方，摘要材料也可来自 OpenAlex。网站展示书目、简短阅读说明与原文链接，不转载完整摘要或正文。

本列表用于文献发现，不保证涵盖所有符合范围的文章。
