CHANGELOG

A record of what’s changed

Features, improvements and selection updates since our public beta began on 4 October 2026.

Dates refer to releases in Beijing time (UTC+8). Routine paper additions are not listed individually.

2026.10.08↗
Added

Nature Physics joins Spotlight

  • Added Nature Physics to the journal whitelist and Spotlight, bringing the collection to fourteen journals and ten Spotlight journals. The same scientific inclusion criteria apply.
  • Reviewed all 47 Nature Physics candidates from 1 September through 8 October and included one paper on percolation and active turbulence. Earlier papers remain in the collection.
2026.10.07↗
AddedImproved

Changelog and clearer collection updates

  • Spotlight cards now show author bylines. Longer lists display the first four names and “et al.”; the full list is available on hover and in paper details.
  • Previously included accepted manuscripts receive a “Published · previously accepted” label when formally published, retaining both dates. Update counts distinguish new additions from these status changes, with one entry per DOI.
  • Added this bilingual changelog, recording important website changes since the public beta began on 4 October. Navigation and footer links provide access.
  • A collection-wide update summary appears above the reading pick. Use its button or the Dates “Update” option to browse newly included and newly published manuscripts from that update, including older papers collected later.
  • Paper cards now use navy journal headers with white reading areas. Spotlight entries in the full collection have a small identifying label.

2026.10.06↗
AddedImprovedFixedData & rules

A daily reading pick, with clearer paper cards

  • AI’s Suggestion adds one pick to each completed daily update, explaining why it is worth reading and what readers can reuse. The first pick was revised after full-text reading; future picks require reading the full paper.
  • Paper cards use slate-blue journal headers and white reading areas. Journal names use full titles except PNAS; Chaos uses its short title.
  • The journal filter again follows the Spotlight sequence, restoring PNAS to its original position.
  • Daily collection rechecks the overlap after the previous search. Older records are checked against review history instead of discarded solely by date; accepted manuscripts are matched before adding a paper.

October coverage and refined selection guidance

  • Added the reviewed 1–5 October collection while retaining September papers, allowing both months to be browsed together.
  • Clarified the scope of network-science contributions and identified editor-selected related readings. Six papers were included through editorial reconsideration, with earlier decisions retained.
  • “View all Spotlight papers” now covers all collected dates instead of retaining the last-30-days filter.

2026.10.04↗
Public beta

NetSci Observatory opens for public beta

  • Opened the public, registration-free site at netsciobs.com, with English and Chinese interfaces and a network-inspired site icon.
  • Published 122 reviewed September papers from thirteen journals. Nine journals form Spotlight, with the latest six papers on the homepage; the full list includes all thirteen.
  • The launch version includes topic browsing, title/author/journal/DOI search, date and journal filters, bilingual reading notes, and links to publisher pages and review records. Accepted manuscripts are identified separately.
  • Added Cloudflare Web Analytics for basic visits and page performance, with bilingual disclosure on the About page.
